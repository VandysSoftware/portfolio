Ask the bartender

What does a project cost?

It depends on scope, which is exactly why the SOW exists. After a short conversation I'll put a fixed price and timeline in writing before anyone commits to anything.

How long does a build take?

A marketing site runs a few weeks. A full custom application runs a few months. I'd rather give you an honest range up front than a fast number I have to walk back.

Will you work on software someone else built?

Yes — that's a good share of the work. I'll start with a short review of what's there and tell you plainly whether it's worth repairing or replacing.

What happens after launch?

Defects are covered during the warranty window named in your SOW. Past that, you can bring me back for changes as needed or set up a monthly retainer if you'd rather have standing time on the calendar.

Do you sign NDAs?

Happily, on request, before we get into specifics. Confidentiality is also built into the MSA.

Are you local?

Based in Kansas City and glad to meet in person. Most work happens remotely, so location isn't a requirement on your end.

What'll it be?

Tell me what you're trying to build. No charge for the conversation.
